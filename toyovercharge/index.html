How it plays

Summon & merge

Drop toys onto a 6×4 board and drag twins together to build stronger ones, all the way to level 5.

Overcharge

Tap a toy with two or more twins and they snap together in a burst of lightning for extra levels and bonus damage.

Mythic Strike

Fill the charge meter and call in Omega Rex, Volcano Phoenix, Golden Emperor or Chronos Titan.

Beat the clock

10 bosses across 5 toy worlds, each with a trick to counter. Then Round 2 brings tougher bosses and bigger rewards.

Good to know

  • No account, no login. Your progress is saved on your device.
  • Free to play with ads. A banner appears from Stage 3, and a video ad may sometimes appear between stages after a win. Reward videos (like Continue +20s) are always optional.
  • One optional purchase: Remove Ads, a one-time purchase that removes the banner and the between-stage video ads.
  • Reminders are opt-in. Daily Gift reminders are local notifications that stay off unless you turn them on.
  • Designed for players aged 13 and up. Currently available in the United States on Android and iPhone.
