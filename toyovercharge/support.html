Support

Need help with Toy Overcharge, or have feedback? We'd love to hear from you.

Email kimsol@solmatter.kr

To help us answer quickly, please include:

  • your phone model and its Android or iOS version;
  • the app version, shown at the bottom of the game's Settings (for example "Toy Overcharge v1.0.0");
  • what happened and on which stage, plus a screenshot if you can;
  • for purchase questions: the Google Play order number (it starts with "GPA.") or the date and amount from your Apple receipt.

Tip: Settings → CONTACT SUPPORT in the game opens an email with the app version already filled in. We usually reply within a few business days (Korea time).

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
  • Summon: spend energy to drop a new toy from your squad onto the 6×4 board.
  • Merge: drag a toy onto an identical toy (same toy, same level) to make one toy a level higher, up to level 5. Dropping onto a different toy swaps them. Dragging a toy onto the dock recycles it for a little energy.
  • Overcharge: tap a toy that has two or more identical twins. They snap into it with lightning: 3 toys give +1 level, 4–5 toys give +2 levels, plus bonus damage and charge.
  • Mythic Strike: when the charge meter is full, tap it to call in your Mythic.
  • Frontline: toys in the row nearest the boss deal 1.5× damage.
  • Defeat the boss before the timer reaches zero. Faster wins earn more stars.
How do I deal with the boss's tricks?
  • Goo covers a toy: tap it 3 times to pop it.
  • Bomb lands on the board: tap it to kick it back at the boss before it explodes.
  • Iron block fills a cell: tap it 4 times to smash it (and earn energy).
  • Shield makes the boss immune: tap the boss to crack the shield.
  • Dark orb sits on your strongest toy: tap it in time, or that toy loses a level.

Every skill is shown a moment before it happens, so watch for the warning.

What happens after Stage 10?

You start Round 2 from Stage 1. Bosses have more HP, and coin rewards are bigger. Each later Round is tougher again. Keep upgrading in the Workshop!

I'm stuck on a stage. Any tips?
  • Spend coins in the Workshop: Attack, Energy and Charge upgrades apply to every battle.
  • Try a different Squad. Supporters like Bunny Totem and Penguin Cryo boost the toys next to them.
  • Keep your strongest toys in the front row, and save your Mythic Strike for the final push.
  • If time runs out, you can choose to watch a short video to continue with +20 seconds (once per attempt).
I bought Remove Ads. Why can I still see a video?

Remove Ads removes the banner and the between-stage video ads. Reward videos, like "Continue +20s", are still offered because some players like them, but they only play if you choose to watch one.

If you still see the banner or between-stage ads, open Settings → RESTORE PURCHASES while connected to the internet, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

How do I restore Remove Ads on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Sign in to the store with the same Apple ID or Google account, open the game, and tap Settings → RESTORE PURCHASES. The No Ads screen also has a "Restore purchases" button. The game often restores it automatically on launch.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are processed by the stores, so refunds are handled by them:

  • App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: see Google Play's refund help page
My progress is gone / can I move my progress to a new phone?

Your progress is saved only on your device. There is no account or cloud save in this version. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes the progress, and we can't restore it. Your phone's own device backup may bring it back when you restore that backup. Remove Ads can always be restored (see above).

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Use Settings → Reminders in the game. If you said "Don't allow" earlier, turn notifications on in your phone's settings first:

  • iPhone: Settings → Notifications → Toy Overcharge
  • Android: Settings → Apps → Toy Overcharge → Notifications

Reminders are created on your phone (for example "Your Daily Gift is ready!") and send no data.

How often will I see ads?
  • No ads during the first tutorial stages. A banner appears at the bottom from Stage 3.
  • A full-screen video ad can appear only between stages after a win, once you have at least 4 wins, at most every second win and at least 2½ minutes apart. Never during a battle.
  • Reward videos only play when you tap "Continue +20s".
"Video isn't ready yet". What should I do?

Videos need an internet connection and sometimes take a few seconds to load. Wait a moment and tap again, or choose Try Again.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If a privacy message applies where you live, the game's Settings shows a PRIVACY CHOICES button where you can review or change your choices, including opting out of the sale or sharing of personal information. On Android you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Do I need an account? How do I delete my data?

No account is needed, and we don't keep any of your data on our servers (we don't have any). Uninstalling the game removes everything it saved on your phone. Ads and purchases need an internet connection. For information that Google's ad service collects, see the Privacy Policy.

Who is the game for?

Toy Overcharge is designed for players aged 13 and up. It is not directed to children under 13.
