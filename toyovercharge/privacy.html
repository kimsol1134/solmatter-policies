Privacy Policy

Toy Overcharge (Android and iOS) · Effective date: October 15, 2026 · Applies to version 1.0 and later until updated

This Privacy Policy explains how SOLMATTER ((주)솔매터, "SOLMATTER", "we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Toy Overcharge (the "App"), distributed on Google Play and the Apple App Store. If you have any question, contact us at kimsol@solmatter.kr.

The short version

  • No account, no login, no chat. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Your game progress stays on your device. We do not run game servers and do not receive your progress.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob. Google collects device and usage information to show and measure ads. You can make privacy choices in the App's Settings when they apply, and in your device settings.
  • The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.
  • Reminder notifications are optional, are created on your device, and send no information.
  • The App is not directed to children under 13.

1. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following on your device using the game engine's local storage (Unity PlayerPrefs):

  • game progress: current stage and round, stars, coins, Workshop upgrades, unlocked toys and Mythics, and your squad;
  • settings: music, sound, and whether reminders are on;
  • whether you own Remove Ads;
  • tutorial and Daily Gift status, plus simple counters (number of sessions, total play time, install date) used only inside the App.

The App does not send this information to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data. Your device's own backup service (for example iCloud or Google device backup) may include it in device backups, depending on your settings. We cannot access those backups and cannot recover lost progress.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), provided by Google LLC, to show a banner ad, occasional full-screen ads between stages, and optional reward videos that you choose to watch. When ads are requested or shown, the SDK collects information and sends it to Google, such as:

  • device and advertising identifiers: on Android, the Advertising ID and app set ID. On iOS, an identifier for vendor. The App does not ask for permission to track you and does not access the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • IP address, which Google may use to estimate your general (approximate) location;
  • ad and app interactions, such as ads shown and tapped and app launches;
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as crash and ad-loading data;
  • device information, such as model, operating system version, language and network type.

Google uses this information to show ads, to personalize them where permitted, to measure them, to prevent fraud and abuse, and to improve its services. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Advertising partners that take part in Google's ad auctions may also receive information. SOLMATTER does not receive your identifiers. We see only aggregated reports, such as the number of ad impressions and earnings.

3. Your choices

  • Privacy Choices in the App. Google's User Messaging Platform determines whether a consent or opt-out message applies where you are. When it does, the App's Settings shows a PRIVACY CHOICES button. There you can review or change your choices at any time, including opting out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising under US state privacy laws.
  • Android: in your device settings (Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads, depending on the device), you can reset or delete your Advertising ID. Deleting it stops its use for personalized ads.
  • iOS: the App does not use App Tracking Transparency, so ads on iOS are not personalized using the IDFA.
  • Remove Ads: buying Remove Ads stops the banner and between-stage ads. Reward videos are shown only when you choose to watch one.
  • Notifications: off unless you turn them on (see section 5).

4. In-app purchase

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not receive your name, payment card or billing address. To unlock Remove Ads, the App asks the store whether your store account owns it, including when you tap Restore Purchases. The result is stored on your device. Apple and Google give us aggregated sales and tax reports.

5. Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device, for example when your Daily Gift is ready. They are created on your device. No push service or server is involved, and no information is sent. You can turn reminders off in the App's Settings or in your device's notification settings.

6. Analytics and app statistics

The App does not include any third-party analytics SDK, and it does not send gameplay events to us. We use the aggregated statistics that Apple and Google provide to all developers, such as installs, active devices, retention, crash rates and store page views. On iOS, these statistics come only from users who have agreed in their device settings to share analytics with app developers. These reports do not identify you.

7. Game engine

The App is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may receive limited technical information about the device, such as device model, operating system version and hardware capabilities, to support and improve the engine. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

8. Emailing us

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include, such as your device model or screenshots. We use it only to answer you and to keep a record of the request. We delete it when it is no longer needed, or sooner if you ask.

9. What we do not do

  • We do not create user accounts or profiles.
  • We do not sell personal information for money.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data.
  • The App has no chat and no user-generated content.

11. Your US state privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana and other states with consumer privacy laws), you may have the right to:

  • know about and access the personal information collected about you, and get a portable copy;
  • correct or delete it;
  • opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising;
  • not be discriminated against for using these rights.

Sale and sharing. We do not sell personal information for money. However, AdMob's use of device identifiers for personalized advertising may be considered a "sale", "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some state laws. You can opt out with PRIVACY CHOICES in the App's Settings (shown where it applies) and with your device's ad settings (see Your choices). We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

How to make a request. Email kimsol@solmatter.kr with the subject "Privacy request". We hold no personal information about you on our own systems, so for most requests we will explain what the App stores locally and how to reach Google about information it holds. We respond within 45 days. You may use an authorized agent. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying with "Appeal" within 30 days, and we will answer within 60 days. If you are not satisfied with the result of an appeal, you may contact your state Attorney General.

12. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and because the App has no accounts and we run no servers, we do not hold such information ourselves. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has used the App, contact us at kimsol@solmatter.kr. We will help you limit advertising data, for example by deleting or resetting the device's advertising ID and uninstalling the App, and we will take any further steps the law requires.

13. Security and retention

The ad SDK and the stores send information over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Information saved on your device is protected by your device's own security. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We keep support emails only as long as needed (see section 8). We keep no other personal information.

14. Where information is processed

SOLMATTER is based in the Republic of Korea. The App is currently offered in the United States. Google, Apple and Unity may process information in the United States and other countries under their own privacy policies.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the App adds features or services. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. If changes are significant, we will also say so in the App's store listing or in the App.
